⭐ First-time Chicago “must-do” checklist Reference

A quick list to revisit during the trip. You do not need to complete everything — the items marked ✓ are already built into the itinerary.

  • ✓ Architecture river cruise — probably the most distinctly Chicago visitor experience.
  • ✓ Chicago Riverwalk — central, easy, and worth doing both by day and after dark.
  • ✓ Millennium Park / Cloud Gate (“The Bean”).
  • ○ One observation deck — Skydeck Chicago or 360 CHICAGO; choose one, preferably on a clear day.
  • ✓ Museum Campus / lakefront — choose Field Museum or Shedd rather than trying to do everything.
  • ○ Art Institute of Chicago — high-priority optional museum if you have a spare half-day.
  • ✓ Lincoln Park / lakefront and ✓ Chinatown — neighborhood experiences beyond the Loop.
  • ✓ Live Chicago sports — Bulls vs Suns is already booked.
  • ○ Chicago food classics — deep-dish pizza, Italian beef, Chicago-style hot dog, or simply a strong local restaurant.
  • ○ Navy Pier — iconic but lower priority for this particular itinerary.

🚆 Optional trips outside Chicago Only if we feel like it

The itinerary is already full, and Northwestern in Evanston is technically outside Chicago. These are optional escapes rather than things we should force into the plan.

  • Oak Park — easiest half-day: CTA Green Line reaches Oak Park directly. Frank Lloyd Wright’s Home & Studio is open daily, and the surrounding historic district has a major concentration of Wright-designed houses.
  • Milwaukee — realistic full-day second city: Amtrak Hiawatha takes about 1 h 29 min from Chicago Union Station to downtown Milwaukee, with several daily round trips. This would require replacing a full Chicago day.
  • Indiana Dunes National Park — nature option: South Shore trains stop inside/near the national park, but many trail/beach areas still require walking from the stations. Best only if the weather is excellent and you specifically want a nature day.

Best fit for this trip: Oak Park if we suddenly have a free half-day. Milwaukee only if we decide we really want a second city. Indiana Dunes is more weather-dependent in October.

🏮 Chinatown day Oct 8

Chicago Chinatown is now a proper itinerary stop rather than just a food suggestion.

  • Use the CTA Red Line to Cermak–Chinatown.
  • Walk from the Chinatown Gate to Chinatown Square, then continue to Ping Tom Memorial Park.

  • After lunch, return north for Lincoln Park / Old Town rather than trying to add another distant neighborhood.
